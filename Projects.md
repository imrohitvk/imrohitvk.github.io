### Spandan
**Jun 2025 – Present**  
*Associated with Indian Institute of Technology, Ropar*

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, OpenAI Whisper, Open Source LLMs  
**Repo:** [vicharanashala/poll-question-gen](https://github.com/vicharanashala/poll-question-gen){:target="_blank"}

A live classroom interaction platform that transforms passive lectures into active learning. Spandan captures teacher speech in real-time, converts it into transcripts via ASR, automatically generates relevant questions using LLMs, and presents them to students through a dedicated portal for instant interaction and assessment.

**Highlights:**
- Real-time speech capture and transcription using OpenAI Whisper
- Automatic quiz question generation from live lecture content
- Student answer portal with live engagement tracking
- Teacher dashboard with session analytics and participation monitoring

### Vi-SlideS
**Dec 2025 – Present**  
*Associated with Indian Institute of Technology, Ropar*

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, LLMs, NLP, WebSockets  
**Repo:** [vicharanashala/vi-slides](https://github.com/vicharanashala/vi-slides){:target="_blank"}

An AI-powered adaptive classroom platform that makes teaching question-driven. After a brief topic introduction, students submit questions that shape the class direction. AI analyses collective questions to provide teachers real-time insights into class mood, motivation, and conceptual understanding. Straightforward questions are auto-answered by AI; complex queries are routed to the teacher's prioritised dashboard.

**Highlights:**
- AI analysis of student questions for mood, motivation, and cognitive complexity
- Smart triage: instant AI responses for simple questions, teacher escalation for complex ones
- Real-time question flow with anonymous/identified submission options
- Class mood and knowledge-gap detection via NLP sentiment analysis

### Peer Evaluation System
**Aug 2024 – Jul 2025**  
*Associated with Indian Institute of Technology, Ropar*

**Tech Stack:** React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, JWT/OAuth  
**Repo:** [vicharanashala/pes](https://github.com/vicharanashala/pes){:target="_blank"}

An intelligent multi-level peer assessment system facilitating peer-to-peer evaluation with built-in quality control. The system assigns unique IDs to each student per quiz for anonymity. Teachers can bulk download QR-coded IDs for offline distribution or allow students to upload directly via portal with auto-generated IDs. Algorithm-based anomaly detection (statistical outlier analysis) flags suspicious patterns to Teaching Assistants, who can escalate to teachers, maintaining academic integrity throughout.

**Highlights:**
- Anonymous evaluation via unique student IDs with optional QR code support
- Statistical anomaly detection flagging suspicious peer evaluations
- Multi-level escalation: peer → automated detection → TA review → teacher intervention
- Rubric-based evaluation, comprehensive analytics, and complete audit trails
